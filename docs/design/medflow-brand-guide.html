01 — Brand

MedFlow within the DHL brand

MedFlow is a decision-support platform built for DHL. It is an endorsed product identity: DHL leads, MedFlow names the solution. The DHL brand carries the trust; MedFlow carries the function.

Everything visual defers to DHL's system — yellow and red, the Delivery typeface, DHL's tone of voice. MedFlow adds only what a data product needs on top: a small product mark, a set of functional status colours, and a monospace face for machine-generated values. Nothing here overrides the DHL guidelines; where the two ever conflict, DHL wins.

P1

Explainable over clever

Every recommendation can be replayed and audited. No black boxes on a medical floor.

P2

Human in the loop

MedFlow proposes; the DHL planner commits. People keep the final say over what moves.

P3

Measured in seconds

Value is picker travel saved and stockouts prevented — reported in real units, not scores.

Source of truth: the DHL Brand Hub is authoritative for the DHL logo, exact colour tokens, the Delivery typeface, and the official Co- and Additional Branding rules. This guide only defines MedFlow's side of the system. Always place official DHL master assets — never recreate the DHL logo.

Do

  • Lead with the DHL logo; keep its clear space per DHL guidelines.
  • Use the divider to separate DHL from MedFlow.

Don't

  • Merge the two into a single combined symbol.

Functional extension — stock status (UC2)

A data product needs colours that mean something. These are a deliberate, documented exception to the brand palette: they carry operational meaning, not brand identity, and appear only inside data components (pills, cells, charts) — never as page colour. Red doubles as "critical", which stays consistent with DHL red.

04 — Typography

Type system

DHL's corporate typeface is Delivery — use it wherever it is licensed and available. Because Delivery is proprietary and can't be embedded in an open build, MedFlow's dashboard specifies Mulish as the approved open-source substitute: a humanist sans with a similar warmth and a full weight range. IBM Plex Mono handles machine-generated values.

05 — Voice

How MedFlow speaks

MedFlow follows DHL's tone of voice — clear, human, on eye level — and adds the discipline a decision tool needs: state what happened, state what to do, quantify it, don't dramatise. Interface copy is sentence case with active verbs.

Write it like this
Stock below reorder point. Transfer 40 units from central to Subsidiary 3.
Recommended: move SKU-04182 to B04. Saves ~20s per pick.

Specific, quantified, action-first.

Not like this
Uh oh! Looks like you might be running a little low on something 😅
Our smart AI thinks this could maybe be a better spot!

Vague, hyped, emoji, hedging.
